Growth strategy

From 2 sign languages to 11, and from an app to a platform.

  1. Phase 1 · Q3 2027

    Grow and pilot

    • Expand vocabulary to 300+ JSL / MSL signs
    • Launch pilots with regional Deaf associations
    • Incorporate and secure institutional growth capital
  2. Phase 2 · Q4 2028

    Open the platform

    • Launch a pay-as-you-go API
    • Banks, clinics and taxis integrate kotoSay natively
  3. Phase 3 · Q1 2030

    Scale across Asia

    • Train our core ML pipeline across 11 Asian sign languages
