Validation

Tested, refined and validated with the Deaf community.

  1. 1 Test

    Evaluated with 8 MSL researchers at UKM. They found limits with fast and distant signs.

  2. 2 Refine

    Refined the model to reach a 1.5 m functional range during rapid signing.

  3. 3 Validate

    A Deaf worker at the RC Deaf Missions (RCDM) café praised its faster speed, instant voice and expanded range.

What’s next

RCDM’s co-founder expressed strong interest in a long-term co-creation partnership to grow kotoSay into a Relay Interpreting Services platform.
