## Notes on wording vs. source

- **Banking.** Supported. In section 3(1) of the DPI Japan letter, a customer was told
  they couldn't open an account without a companion (付き添いがいないと口座開設できない).
- **Police · Yokohama.** The Yokohama case says that at a traffic accident, police heard
  from hearing people first and left the Deaf person until later (ろうあ者が後回しになる),
  and said providing a sign language interpreter would be difficult. That is softer than
  "refused to take a statement".
- **Mr. Ong.** The NST article describes him as an **e-hailing** driver. The slide says
  "taxi driver", following the brainstorm doc.
